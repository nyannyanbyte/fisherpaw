Privacy Policy

October 28, 2026 | FisherPaw: Bridge Defenders
Summary: FisherPaw does not collect or store any directly identifiable personal information (PII) such as names, phone numbers, or emails. All game progress and items are stored strictly on the user’s local device.

1. Overview

Developer nyannyanbyte ("Company") prioritizes user privacy and strictly complies with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable data privacy regulations.

2. Information Collected

FisherPaw does not require account creation or login, and does not transmit personal data to remote developer servers. However, for in-game advertisements, third-party SDKs (Google AdMob) may automatically process non-personally identifiable information:

  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, screen size, carrier, language settings.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID).
  • Ad Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, and completion logs.
  • Network Information: Coarse/approximate location based on IP.

3. Purpose of Collection

  • Delivering rewarded and interstitial ads via Google AdMob.
  • Service quality improvement, crash diagnostics, and fraud prevention.

4. Third-Party SDKs

Google AdMob (Google LLC) - Mobile advertising delivery and performance measurement.

5. Data Storage and Deletion

Game data (gold, stage records, skins, upgrades) is stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

6. Children’s Privacy

FisherPaw does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, and applies appropriate advertising content ratings.
