Account & Data Deletion Request

FisherPaw: Bridge Defenders
Google Play Data Safety Notice: FisherPaw does not use an online account system. All game data is stored exclusively on your device.

Method 1: Uninstall App (Instant Permanent Deletion)

Tap and hold the FisherPaw app icon on your device and select "Uninstall" or "Delete". All local data is instantly and permanently wiped.

Method 2: Clear Storage via Android Settings

Go to Settings > Apps > FisherPaw > Storage > tap "Clear Data" and "Clear Cache" to reset the game to factory state.

Inquiries and Email Data Deletion Request

If you have contacted us via email and wish to have email records removed, send a request to contact@nyannyanbyte.com. All correspondence records will be deleted within 48 hours.
